# Cost-Effective Treatment of Wastewater

## Project Overview
This project focuses on researching and developing affordable, sustainable wastewater treatment solutions for residential societies, small industries, hotels, and other establishments.

## Objectives
- Reduce wastewater treatment and operating costs.
- Explore biological treatment technologies such as Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR).
- Investigate biochar-based filtration and decentralized greywater treatment.
- Improve treated wastewater reuse for suitable non-potable applications.
- Evaluate energy consumption, chemical requirements, sludge generation, and overall treatment costs.

## Proposed Development
1. Study wastewater characteristics and treatment requirements.
2. Compare conventional and cost-effective treatment technologies.
3. Develop preliminary process designs and material balances.
4. Evaluate treatment efficiency and operating expenses.
5. Identify opportunities for pilot-scale testing and future implementation.

## Current Status
Research and development stage. Treatment performance and cost-effectiveness will need to be validated through experiments and pilot-scale testing.

## Long-Term Vision
To develop practical, scalable, and economically viable wastewater treatment solutions that support water conservation and environmental sustainability.
